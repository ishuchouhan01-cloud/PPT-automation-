HOW TO USE
==========

1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you can write to (Documents is fine; avoid a read-only
   network share, because the application saves a copy of each deck beside itself).

2. Double-click START.bat. A black window opens and stays open. Your browser opens at
   http://127.0.0.1:3000 — if it does not, type that address in yourself.

3. Choose the PROJECT TYPE at the top of the page:
       Launch & Design      or      Design & Build
   The upload boxes change to match your choice.

4. Upload the files for that project type — drag and drop, or click a box.

5. Confirm the project the application detected, and enter the PROJECT CODE.
   Entering the code matters: it is how the application tells your project apart from
   every other project in the Summary Dashboard.

6. Click Generate. It takes a few seconds to a minute.

7. Click Download PPTX.


WHICH FILES TO UPLOAD
---------------------

LAUNCH & DESIGN
  ASTA Excel export (.xlsx/.xlsm)      REQUIRED. The schedule export for this project.
  Summary Dashboard (.xlsx)            Needed for Red Flag Summary, Key Focus Area and
                                       the Tracker & Schedule slide.
  Previous Weekly PPT (.pptx)          Supplies the cover, Land Acquisition, Master Plan
                                       and any extra slides carried forward.
  Land Acquisition update              Only if that slide changed this week. A .pptx
                                       containing the new slide, or an image. Left out,
                                       it carries over from the previous deck.

DESIGN & BUILD
  Design & Build Excel export view     REQUIRED, and the ONLY schedule file. One export
                                       view holding this project's Design and/or
                                       Construction schedule data. The Design and
                                       Construction branches are found inside it, so there
                                       is no separate construction upload:
                                         - a "Design" branch      -> Project Overview
                                         - a "Construction" branch -> Project Overview -
                                           Construction, and Schedule - Construction
                                         - both branches           -> both slides, 4A and 4B
                                         - construction absent     -> those slides are simply
                                           left out. Nothing else is asked for.
  Summary Dashboard (.xlsx)            Needed for Red Flag Summary, Key Focus Area and
                                       both timelines.
  Previous Weekly PPT (.pptx)          Supplies the cover and the Site Progress pages.
                                       Site photographs are never generated — they can
                                       only come from your previous deck.


WHAT YOU GET
------------
An editable .pptx, plus a validation report listing what was built, what was left out and
why. A section whose input is missing is left out of the deck AND out of Contents rather
than being filled with something else.

The cover's "Weekly Update" date is set from the reporting date for this run, not from the
date on the previous deck.


TO STOP
-------
Close the black window, or press Ctrl+C in it.
